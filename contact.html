Common questions

Can I pick more than three colors?
The standard blankets top out at three — it keeps the pattern clean and the price predictable. Ask me if you have something bigger in mind.
How long does it take?
3–4 weeks from confirmed order to shipped, longer for a Queen or during the holidays. I'll give you a real date before you pay.
How do I pay?
Send an order request from the designer, I confirm the details, then you get a secure payment link. No charge until we've both agreed on the blanket.
Can I return it?
Each blanket is made specifically for you, so returns are limited — but if something arrives damaged or isn't what we agreed on, I'll make it right. Edit this to match the policy you actually want.
Do you ship internationally?
Answer this one based on what you're willing to do.
